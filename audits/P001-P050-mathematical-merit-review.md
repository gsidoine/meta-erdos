# Mathematical merit and formulation review, P001–P050

The catalogue distinguishes a well-defined construction from a significant
mathematical question. The existence of a graph, random variable, matrix,
homology group or optimization parameter does not by itself establish an
interesting open problem. Elementary identities, such as a kernel
characterization of matrix rank, are not independent progress on the
corresponding probability limit. Finite verification does not imply
a uniform theorem.

A retained **candidate** has a definite unresolved structural or
quantitative target; the label does not certify historical novelty.
A **reframe** entry retains its permanent identifier and useful
mathematics but requires a stronger independent statement before being
treated as a research priority. **Needs specification** means that
the core quantified assertion or intended nontrivial asymptotic
scale remains insufficiently focused. An internally proved or
historically known result is not an open conjecture.

The assessment below concerns mathematical consequence as well as
historical overlap. It is a source comparison and mathematical
screening, **not** a claim that the literature of all 50 subjects
has been exhausted. Permanent IDs, archived proofs, counterexamples,
and citations remain unchanged unless a concrete error is documented.

## Changes of mathematical status

Four entries have been downgraded on mathematical grounds, rather
than solely because a matching reference has not been located:

- **P012 and P013 (reframe):** the present questions combine known
  constrained-matching or Sidon models with random deletion. The
  conditional observables are definable, but no specific arithmetic
  reconfiguration phenomenon, scale separation, or rigorous new
  obstruction has yet been demonstrated. A nonmonotone phase-diagram
  request is not by itself a falsifiable threshold conjecture.
- **P022 (needs specification):** the construction is well-defined,
  but a generic request for $H_0,H_1,H_2$ thresholds and $\beta_1$
  fluctuations is too broad; the only supporting example is the
  four-vertex complex at $N=1$. A specific conjectural exponent,
  structurally justified witness family and comparison with
  unimodular triangulation theory are required. Its previously
  imprecise symbolic reward is suspended.
- **P037 (reframe):** the leading induced-cycle asymptotic is
  settled within the repository and unbounded deficit follows
  from existing prime-gap theory. The remaining maximal-deficit
  question has not yet been separated from prime-gap extrema
  into an independent graph-theoretic theorem.

**P011 is retained after sharpening its principal question** to
the isolated-vertex criterion for the matching threshold of
the dependent sum-diagonal model. Nonmonotone regular-factor
saturation is secondary, not another proposed sharp threshold.

P023 remains a candidate, but the equivalence of full rank
with absence of nonzero annihilating parity functionals is
a tautology of linear algebra, **not a mathematical theorem about
its random rank threshold**. Its standing depends on solving
the latter.

## Relevant predecessors requiring exact comparison

- [Godbole–Janson–Locantore–Rapoport, *Random Sidon Sequences*
  (1999)](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1998.2325) treats random
  Sidon existence thresholds, not P013's constrained exchange
  space.
- [Dyer–Jerrum–Müller, *On the switch Markov chain for perfect
  matchings*](https://doi.org/10.1145/2822322) develops the
  general switch-chain problem relevant to P005 and P012.
- [Baur–Bergerova–Voon–Xu, *Flip graphs of coloured triangulations
  of convex polygons* (2024)](https://doi.org/10.12958/adm2312),
  and [Acharya–Mütze–Verciani, *Flips in Colorful Triangulations*
  (2025)](https://doi.org/10.20382/jocg.v16i1a9) are closer to
  P015 than unrestricted Lawson flip connectivity. A connection
  with the simultaneous four-residue parity restriction is
  not yet an equivalence proof.
- [Graham–Rödl–Ruciński, *On Schur Properties of Random Subsets
  of Integers* (1996)](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1996.0155)
  already studies arithmetic hypergraph configurations at
  the $N^{-1/2}$ scale, relevant to P036.
- [Pintz, *On the Ratio of Consecutive Gaps Between Primes*
  (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-22240-0_17)
  supplies the existing prime-gap theorem used in P037.
- [Liu–Pach–Palincza, *The number of maximum primitive sets of
  integers* (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548321000018)
  and [McNew, *Counting primitive subsets and other statistics
  of the divisor graph* (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2020.103237)
  are foundational predecessors for P027's factorization.
- [Wu–Feng, *Rigidity and stability for biased cross-intersecting
  families* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26871)
  supplies a relevant extremal-product-measure interface for
  P040. It does not establish the catalogue's conditional
  deletion infimum without an explicit reduction.

## Resulting scope

Exactly 50 permanent identifiers are retained. The status totals
are **38 candidate, 8 reframe, 2 needs specification, 1 proved
internally, and 1 known result**. The first four labels describe
the status of a formulation, not a numerical rank, claim of
authorship, or measured mathematical impact.

The conditions attached to the symbolic prizes are independently
reviewed. A completion already proved for an isolated component
does not count as an open full-chain theorem; a single counterexample
is enough to disprove a universal existence conjecture. The
submission record and manuscript for P010 remain separate from
any later revision.
