# Literature comparison for P001–P050

This register distinguishes the existence of a mathematical predecessor from identity of the remaining question. A reference establishes only the mathematical point described alongside it. **No novelty certification or universal claim of unresolved status follows from this register.** Source records reproduced from individual entries require independent bibliographic checking before use in a manuscript.

## Directly identifiable prior-art intersections

- **P007:** the coprime-labeling subproblem has a known predecessor. Only the explicitly narrower extension is retained, with formulation *reframe*.
- **P025:** the linear adjacent-lcm bottleneck has a documented predecessor. The surviving object is a cyclic variant, with formulation *reframe*.
- **P009:** the two-prime-palette construction uses a prime difference $2n=p-q$, a recognized Maillet-type problem; see [OEIS A202472](https://oeis.org/A202472). Neither necessity nor equivalence to the original two-palette matching conjecture is proved. The formulation is marked *reframe*.
- **P029:** the complete anti-diagonal X-ray of a permutation is explicitly defined in [Bebeacua–Mansour–Postnikov–Severini (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.endm.2005.05.063). Connectivity of its fibers with prime-supported entries and bounded-size moves is a further restriction not resolved by that definition alone. The formulation is marked *reframe*.
- **P032:** [Erdős Problem 17](https://www.erdosproblems.com/17) asks for primes realizing every bounded even difference, with different quantifiers and prime bounds. The prime-sum diameter question reduces to related uniform difference coverage, but is **not known equivalent** to Problem 17. It is marked *reframe*.

*Reframe* is an editorial statement about overlap and the need for a better-distinguished problem. It does **not** assert that the particular formulation has been answered.

## Baselines and unresolved extensions

- **P010:** an internally checked proof is included in the catalogue. Editorial priority and external refereeing are separate, and the submitted manuscript remains archived unchanged.
- **P020:** general random $p$-adic cokernel laws have a large literature, including [Cheong–Kaplan (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.03.035). The structured Hankel ensemble must be compared with universality hypotheses at theorem level.
- **P031/P035:** classical Sidon extremal and coloring theorems cannot be counted as new results. The simultaneous coprimality or divisibility-antichain constraints are the remaining objects.
- **P037:** the leading induced-cycle asymptotic and unboundedness of the prime-gap deficit are not open objectives. The retained question is the maximal growth order of the deficit.
- **P047:** Mycielski and [Fraser](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08231) establish qualitative and half-dimensional avoidance baselines. [Pach–Zakharov (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00151-5) is a direct predecessor for simultaneous Sidon constraints. Full dimension one over $\mathbb F_4[[T]]$ remains unverified against all specialist sources.
- **P048 (resolved):** the combined coprimality and squarefree-sum existence assertion follows for every $n$ by reflecting successive terminal intervals across primes provided by Bertrand's postulate. This is a direct application of [Greenfield–Greenfield's classical prime-sum reflection](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/green.html) (1998), not a novel open problem. The original reward is withdrawn; the permanent identifier is retained.
- **P049:** [OEIS A013928](https://oeis.org/A013928) explicitly records the full coprimality-matrix rank as a squarefree-count function. The question concerns Bernoulli-induced principal submatrices, not that known identity.
- **P050:** the leading-order chromatic problem already follows from [Östergård's hypercube coloring](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2004.06.010). Exact calculations now prove $\chi(H_4)=8$ and $\chi(H_8)=16$; the remaining dyadic case starts at dimension $16$. The small-case arguments have no certified historical priority.

## Status and limits

The source-facing reclassification changes P009, P029, and P032 from *candidate* to *reframe*. The permanent identifiers are retained. P007 and P025 remain *reframe*, P008 remains *needs specification*, and P010 remains *proved internally*.

Labels asserting *apparent novelty* on the basis of a targeted search are replaced with *not certified; targeted literature comparison only*. Lack of an exact-title match cannot establish newness; only comparison with equivalent theorems and known open formulations can justify a stronger statement.

The catalogue contains **50 numbered entries**: 42 candidates, five requiring reformulation, one needing specification, one internally proved result and one known result (P048). This is a classification of mathematical documentation, not a count of historically novel open problems.
