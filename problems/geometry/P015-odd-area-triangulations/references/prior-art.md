# Nearest literature for P015

1. Baur, Bergerova, Voon and Xu, *Flip graphs of coloured triangulations of convex polygons* ([arXiv:2402.06546](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06546)). Their colored flip model must be compared with the fixed vertex-color restriction here.
2. Acharya, Mütze and Verciani, *Flips in colorful triangulations* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.20382/jocg.v16i1a9), 2025). Check the hypotheses of their connectivity results before inferring an answer for arbitrary four-color boundary sequences.
3. Debrohim, Sasaki and Nunes, cyclically repeated vertex-color triangulation results ([arXiv:2608.26006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26006)). Their cyclic coloring assumptions are not automatically equivalent to arbitrary modulo-two residue sequences.

**Priority warning.** The arithmetic-to-color translation is proved elementary mathematics, not a certified novel reduction. A theorem-level equivalence or counterexample comparison is required before claiming P015 historically new.
