# Nearest literature for P020

1. Dwivedi and Grinberg (2022), finite-field Hankel ranks ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2022.02.014)). Classical rank enumeration, not an original project theorem.
2. Yiasemides (2024), characteristic polynomials and finite-field Hankel kernels ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40687-023-00418-7)). The square-matrix kernel shape used in the partial group laws is a corollary of this source.
3. Wood (2019), random integral cokernels ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1353/ajm.2019.0008)). The distribution for independent random matrices does not automatically apply to Hankel dependence.
4. Jang, Kaplan, Lee and Yu (2026), *A mod $p$ determinant criterion for Cohen–Lenstra convergence of random $p$-adic matrices with prescribed zero patterns* ([arXiv:2606.06993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06993)). Their random entries are independently sampled away from prescribed zeros. A Hankel matrix instead repeats its $2n-1$ independent coefficients along antidiagonals; applicability of these zero-pattern criteria is not established.
5. Cheong and Kaplan (2022), *Generalizations of results of Friedman and Washington on cokernels of random $p$-adic matrices* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.03.035)). These results concern polynomial transforms of entrywise Haar-random matrices rather than the Hankel ensemble.

**Historical novelty and unresolved-status certification are outstanding.** The existing exact theorem notes attribute imported results in their proofs.
