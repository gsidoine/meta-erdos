# Literature comparison for P047

The following results are established predecessors; a negative exact-phrase search does not certify priority.

1. J. Mycielski, *Independent sets in topological algebras*, **Fundamenta Mathematicae** 55 (1964), 139–147, [doi:10.4064/fm-55-2-139-147](https://doi.org/10.4064/fm-55-2-139-147). Its category-theoretic perfect-set theorem applies to countably many nowhere-dense algebraic relations, but does not prescribe Hausdorff dimension.
2. M. Doležal and W. Kubiś, *Perfect independent sets with respect to infinitely many relations*, **Archive for Mathematical Logic** 55 (2016), 847–856, [doi:10.1007/s00153-016-0498-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00153-016-0498-3). Stronger formulations of perfect independence.
3. T. Keleti, *A 1-dimensional subset of the reals that intersects each of its translates in at most a single point*, **Real Analysis Exchange** 24 (1998/99), 843–844, [doi:10.2307/44153003](https://doi.org/10.2307/44153003). Full-dimensional additive Sidon behavior over the real field.
4. A. Máthé, *Sets of large dimension not containing polynomial configurations*, **Advances in Mathematics** 316 (2017), 691–709, [doi:10.1016/j.aim.2017.01.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2017.01.002). Quantitative pattern avoidance for polynomial relations.
5. R. Fraser, *Large subsets of local fields not containing configurations*, [arXiv:1612.08231](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08231). The closest local-field approach; its hypotheses and dimension bounds require a precise comparison with simultaneous sum/product collisions.
6. Y. Jing and A. Mudgal, *Finding large additive and multiplicative Sidon sets in sets of integers*, **Mathematische Annalen** 391 (2025), 685–715, [doi:10.1007/s00208-024-02932-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-024-02932-7). Relevant arithmetic intersection of Sidon conditions.
7. J. C. Oxtoby, *The Banach–Mazur game and the Banach category theorem*, in *Contributions to the Theory of Games*, vol. III, **Annals of Mathematics Studies** 39 (1957), 159–163. [Historical game background](https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Alpha-favourable_space).

**Priority status.** Unverified. In particular, the existing large-dimensional local-field pattern-avoidance results must be checked at theorem level before any claim that the precise dimension-one question is historically open or new. Neither a general game on a hyperspace nor the existence of perfect algebraically independent sets should be presented as new.
